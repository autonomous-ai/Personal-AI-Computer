# Kit assembly manual: corrections

Corrections to the [kit assembly manual](kit-assembly-manual.pdf). Where this page and the PDF disagree, follow this page.

- **Step 1** — the nine standoffs are M3x15mm M/F (the text says M4x15mm).
- **Step 2** — also uses four M4x5mm M/F standoffs and four M4x10mm spacers (the text lists only the four M4x6mm button head screws).
- **Step 5** — the rear panel to bracket is one M4x12mm flat head screw (the text says M4x8mm; the hardware list and drawing say M4x12mm).
- **Page 12** — ignore the leftover "STEP 19 / STEP 20" text. The steps end at 26 and 27.
- **Hardware list** — 26 M4x6mm button head screws are supplied; the steps use 24 (2 spare). Step 12 uses no screws.
